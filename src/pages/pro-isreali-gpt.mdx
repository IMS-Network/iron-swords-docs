# **Habara GPT: Strengthening Our Hasbara Efforts**
</div>

<div style={{ direction: 'ltr' }}>



Our mission is to develop a specialized chatbot that generates high-quality content to support our Hasbara efforts. 

With the release of [ChatGPT’s custom chatbot feature](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpts/), we now have the ability to create an AI assistant specifically designed to enhance our messaging, ensuring factual, compelling, and effective communication.

To maximize the chatbot’s accuracy and effectiveness, we will provide it with a comprehensive database of verified documents, stories, and reports detailing our narrative and the ongoing war. The more extensive and detailed our dataset, the more precise and reliable the chatbot's responses will become.

For example, a document on the terrorist organization **UNRWA** would expose its deep affiliations with **Hamas**, its role in **kidnapping hostages during the October 7 massacre**, and its broader participation in acts of terror. 

Expanding our knowledge base, we ensure that *Habara GPT* remains a powerful tool in the fight against misinformation and anti-Israel propaganda.
